# 🎄 Sleigh Catch

A Christmas arcade game — steer your sleigh across the bottom of the screen, catch falling gifts and cookies, and dodge lumps of coal before you lose all three lives!

**Play it now → https://sleigh-dash-emanfj.vercel.app**

---

## How to play

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Move left / right | Arrow keys or A / D |
| Steer toward a spot | Click or tap anywhere on the screen |
| Dash (quick burst) | Space bar or double-tap |

**Catch** 🎁 gifts (+1 pt) and 🍪 cookies (+3 pts).  
**Dodge** 🪨 coal — each hit costs one ❤️ life.  
Every 10 points the items fall faster and spawn more often.

### Power-ups
- 🧲 **Magnet** — gifts drift toward you for 5 s  
- 🛡️ **Shield** — blocks the next bad hit  
- ☕ **Hot Cocoa** — slows everything down for 5 s  

### Combo streaks
Catch items in a row without missing or getting hit:  
5 catches → ×2 · 10 catches → ×3 · 20 catches → ×5

### Reindeer
Earn a 🦌 reindeer every 25 points (up to 4). Each one widens your gift catch radius by 8 %.

---

## Extra features

- 📸 **Use my face** — pick a photo on the start screen. It is circle-cropped with a Christmas hat and used as your hero. Stored locally in your browser; never sent anywhere.
- 🎄 **Make a postcard** — after game over, download a Christmas card with your score and "God jul!".
- ❄️ Background snow and northern lights (appear from score 50)

---

## Running locally

Open `index.html` directly in your browser — no build step needed.
